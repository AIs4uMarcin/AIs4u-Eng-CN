Privacy Policy.

How we collect, process and protect your personal data when you visit our website or engage our services.

Last updated: 21 May 2026

Plain-English summary: We collect the minimum data needed to respond to your enquiries, run our services, and keep our website working. We never sell your data, never train public AI models on it, and we operate under EU GDPR. If you want your data deleted, email us at contact@aisolution4.you and we will remove it within 30 days.

1. Data controller

The data controller responsible for processing your personal data is AIsolution4.you Sp. z o.o. with its registered office in Poznań, Poland.

Contact: contact@aisolution4.you

2. What personal data we collect

2.1 Data you provide directly

When you contact us via the contact form, by email, or when booking a meeting via Calendly:

  • Name and surname
  • Business email address
  • Company name and role
  • Phone number (optional)
  • The content of your message and any context you share about your project

2.2 Data collected automatically

When you visit our website:

  • IP address (anonymised after 30 days)
  • Browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • Pages visited, time spent, referring URL
  • Country-level geographic location (derived from IP)

We use this for security, debugging and aggregate analytics. We do not use cross-site tracking cookies and do not share this data with advertising networks.

3. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under Article 6 of the GDPR:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): when you engage us for advisory or implementation services.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): for responding to enquiries, website security, and aggregate analytics.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): for any optional communications you have opted in to, such as the newsletter.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): for accounting, tax, and statutory record-keeping.

4. How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare an offer or proposal
  • To deliver the services you contract us for
  • To issue invoices and meet our accounting obligations
  • To improve our website and services through aggregate analytics
  • To send you operational communications about active projects

5. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiry data: 12 months from your last contact, then deleted unless an engagement begins.
  • Client data: for the duration of the engagement and 6 years after, as required by Polish tax and accounting law.
  • Website analytics: 26 months, then deleted.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe.

6. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with carefully selected processors who help us run our business, under written data-processing agreements compliant with Article 28 of the GDPR:

  • Calendly - meeting scheduling (data processed in the EU/USA under EU Standard Contractual Clauses)
  • Google Workspace - email and document collaboration (EU data residency)
  • Our hosting and CDN providers - for serving the website
  • Accounting firm and law firm in Poland - bound by professional secrecy

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not share your data with advertising networks. We do not use your data to train public AI models.

7. International data transfers

Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (e.g. to Calendly servers in the USA), we rely on EU-approved Standard Contractual Clauses and additional safeguards to ensure your data receives equivalent protection.

8. Your rights under GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access - get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification - correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") - request deletion of your data
  • Right to restriction - limit how we process your data
  • Right to data portability - receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object - object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Right to withdraw consent - at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (UODO, uodo.gov.pl) or the supervisory authority in your country of residence

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@aisolution4.you. We respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies and tracking

Our website uses only essential cookies required for the site to function correctly and a minimal first-party analytics cookie. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or social-network fingerprinting.

10. Data security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest
  • Access controls based on the principle of least privilege
  • Closed-environment AI workflows audited by our state-cleared CTO
  • NIS2-aligned operational practices for clients in regulated sectors
  • Regular backups and tested disaster recovery procedures

11. Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for business clients. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via a notice on the website. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version.
