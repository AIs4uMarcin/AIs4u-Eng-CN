How we make digitalization work

Four principles that make AI genuinely productive.

AI is most valuable when it is embedded in operations - not presented as a slide. These four principles guide how we build and run the digital stack for every programme.

AI embedded in operations

We use AI where it directly contributes to speed, coverage or quality - in reception, content production, anomaly detection and attribution. Every AI deployment is tied to a measurable operational outcome.

Data security by design

Your data is never sent to public AI models for training. Every AI workflow runs in a closed environment audited by our state-cleared CTO - with GDPR and NIS2 compliance built in from day one.

AI content that elevates the brand

AI delivers scale for variants, social content and product copy in idiomatic Polish. For hero creative - brand films, voiceovers, key landing pages and customer-facing creators - we use the best Polish actors and voice talent. The result is high quality at scale.

Built for your ownership

CRM, ERP, advertising accounts, dashboards, content libraries and tooling are all set up in your name. The full stack is functional and transferable from day one - with no migration project required at any future point.

The stack we set up

Six layers of digital infrastructure - built for you, owned by you.

Every layer is built on accounts you own with credentials you keep. We are builders rather than gatekeepers - the entire stack stays yours and remains fully functional, regardless of the engagement status.

01

Designated CRM - tailored to your sales motion

We configure a CRM specifically for your Polish programme. Pipeline stages reflect how your sales actually work (distributor, direct and marketplace each follow a different flow). Deal qualification criteria match your ideal customer profile. Automations move records, send alerts and log activities - so your sales team focuses on selling rather than administration.

Stack options: HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, or custom-built on a Polish-friendly database. Your call, our recommendation based on scale and integrations.

02

ERP integration & operations layer

Where invoicing, inventory, fulfilment or warehouse needs to talk to sales: we integrate or set up the ERP module that closes the loop. Polish VAT/OSS, BLIK/P24 payment reconciliation, InPost paczkomaty integration, returns workflows - the operational plumbing that turns deals into shipped revenue.

For smaller programmes, a lightweight ops layer; for larger ones, integration with SAP, Comarch, Symfonia or your global ERP via API.

03

Real-time client dashboards

Single login. Every KPI in one place. Sales pipeline, marketing spend, CAC, LTV, return on investment per channel, and attribution - live. One unified view of the programme, refreshed continuously.

Standard widgets include: pipeline velocity by stage and partner, marketing spend per channel with CAC, contribution margin per cohort, attribution view (first-touch, last-touch and multi-touch), and a KPI tracker against Phase-1 targets. Mobile-friendly, exportable to PDF and shareable with HQ.

04

KPI-linked campaign tracking

Every campaign is connected to a business KPI before launch. Phase 1 produces the written KPI structure, and every campaign brief from that point on cites the KPI it serves. Disciplined campaign tagging. Server-side conversion tracking. A multi-touch attribution model tuned to your sales cycle.

Weekly spend and return per channel and per campaign, visible on the dashboard. Every campaign is anchored to a measurable revenue contribution.

05

AI reception, AI content + real actors

AI reception: every inbound call to your Polish line is answered, recorded, transcribed and ranked by lead quality in minutes. Hot leads routed to sales immediately. 24/7 coverage, Polish & English, GDPR-compliant.

AI content + real talent: AI generates volume content (variants, social, product copy) at 10× the speed in idiomatic Polish. Real Polish actors, voice talent and creators handle hero content (brand films, voiceovers, key landing pages). The boundary is creative, not budgetary.

06

Closed-environment AI, audited by our CTO

Your client lists, pricing strategy and sales conversations stay inside your environment, fully isolated from public AI models. We run AI inside closed environments - private deployments of open models, or enterprise contracts with full data isolation. Every integration is reviewed by our CTO Karol (cleared at Polish state-security level) before it touches client data.

GDPR-compliant by design. NIS2-aligned for clients in regulated sectors. The compliance work runs quietly in the background - protecting you against regulatory exposure that can run into millions of euros.
