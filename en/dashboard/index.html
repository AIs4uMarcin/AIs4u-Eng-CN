What’s in every panel

Four deep views - one for each side of the operation.

From the Overview you drill into any of the four panels. Each one shows what the people running that part of the business need to see - and gives HQ enough context to ask the right questions on the weekly call.

Sales

Pipeline · Conversion · Partners

The view your country manager opens every morning. Every deal in every stage, every partner, every win and every stalled conversation - with the math that matters: how fast is the pipeline moving, how much will close, who is selling what.

  • Pipeline by stage & partner - live deal value, count, weighted by probability
  • Win rate & deal velocity - by sector, segment and channel, vs your baseline
  • Top distributors & key accounts - revenue, growth, last contact, next step
  • Revenue vs target - MTD, QTD, YTD with the Phase-1 KPI line visible
  • Hot leads from AI reception - ranked, transcribed, routed in <5 minutes
  • Forecast - weighted pipeline closing this month & next

Marketing

Spend · ROI · Attribution

Every złoty spent and every euro returned, tracked to the campaign that earned it. No more separate agency PDFs that disagree on the numbers - one attribution model, one source of truth, refreshed continuously.

  • Spend per channel - Google, Meta, LinkedIn, native/RTB, SEO & GEO investment
  • Return per channel - attributed revenue, ROAS, contribution margin
  • CAC & LTV cohort view - blended and by channel, weekly cohorts
  • Multi-touch attribution - first-touch, last-touch and weighted - reconciled
  • Campaign ↔ KPI mapping - every campaign linked to the business KPI it serves
  • Content production - AI volume content + real Polish actors on hero assets

Logistics

Fulfilment · Inventory · Returns

The operational side that decides whether the sales effort actually produces shipped revenue. Stock positions, fulfilment SLA, returns, marketplace performance - everything that connects an order in Poland to a shipped product in your customer’s hands.

  • Fulfilment SLA - on-time rate, late shipments, root-cause flagging
  • Inventory & stock alerts - safety-stock breaches, slow-movers, days of cover
  • Returns & complaints - rate, reasons, by SKU, with trend lines
  • Marketplace performance - Allegro, Empik, Mall - orders, ratings, buy-box share
  • Paczkomaty & courier mix - InPost, DPD, DHL splits with cost per shipment
  • Cross-border & OSS - VAT thresholds, customs holdups for EU-wide shipping

Performance & Operations

SLA · KPI tracker · Health

The control tower for the whole programme. Phase-1 KPIs in one place, every commitment made by us shown against actuals, success-bonus thresholds visible - so HQ can see at a glance whether the programme is on, ahead or behind.

  • KPI tracker - live status against the Phase-1 targets, success-bonus lines visible
  • Programme health - trend lines, anomaly alerts, AI-flagged risks
  • AI reception performance - call volumes, lead-quality scoring, response times
  • Team activity - what we shipped this week, what’s queued, who’s on what
  • Compliance & security - GDPR & NIS2 status, audit trail, access logs
  • Invoices & bonus calc - Fixed Cost + Success Bonus breakdown, transparent math
Built for HQ from abroad

Designed for the executives who never land in Warsaw.

Most of the people who need this dashboard most are sitting in Stockholm, Munich, Milan, London or New York. The product is built for their reality - time-zone, currency, language, and the fact they’re reading it on a phone in a taxi.

Multi-currency, side-by-side

Every monetary value shown in PLN and your HQ currency: EUR, USD, GBP, SEK, NOK, CHF, JPY. FX rates refreshed daily, exchange-rate impact isolated from underlying business performance.

Your time zone, our timestamps

All timestamps localised to your time zone, with the Warsaw time shown alongside. “Synced 12 minutes ago” means twelve minutes ago for you, not for someone else.

English & Polish, side-by-side

Toggle the language for any view. Polish KPI names, distributor names and channel names stay in their original Polish form - so what you read matches what your local team says.

Phone-first, not phone-tolerant

The full feature set works on phone: KPIs, charts, drill-downs, exports. Read it in a taxi, share a screenshot with your CFO, drill into a number while waiting for boarding.

Board-pack export, one click

Auto-generated monthly board pack as PDF: KPI tracker, narrative commentary written by our team, top wins and risks. Branded for your company - ready to circulate without editing.

Share without sharing the world

Granular access: CEO sees everything, CFO sees finance & logistics, board sees the summary. Read-only links for advisors, time-limited shares for due diligence and investor reviews.

How it’s built

One platform, your data, your accounts.

The dashboard pulls live data from the systems we set up in your name - CRM, ERP, ad accounts, GA4, marketplace APIs - into a unified view. Built on accounts you own, with credentials you keep, audited by our state-cleared CTO.

Live data, every source

The dashboard reads from the systems where work actually happens: your CRM, your ERP, your ad accounts, GA4, marketplace APIs, AI reception logs. No copy-paste, no monthly file dumps, no agency-PDF bottleneck.

Where a system doesn’t have an API we build the integration. Where data formats clash we reconcile them. The dashboard hides all of that - what you see is one consistent view.
