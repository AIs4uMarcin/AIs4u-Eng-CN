How our operating model is built

Four principles built into every engagement.

Our operating model is designed around four principles that consistently produce better outcomes for our clients. These are the working agreements we make from day one.

Defined timelines, in writing

Phase 1 produces a written 90-day roadmap with milestones documented upfront. If anything needs to shift, you hear it in the weekly update with full context — not as a quarterly surprise.

Transparent reporting

We build dashboards on accounts you own, with live data, weekly written commentary, monthly executive reviews, and quarterly business reviews with HQ. The same numbers, visible to everyone, at all times.

Fixed scope, fixed cost

The Fixed Cost is exactly that — fixed. Any out-of-scope work is documented, priced and approved in writing before it begins. Your CFO can plan with full confidence in the monthly number.

Transferable on day one

Your CRM, ERP, ad accounts, dashboards, content libraries and tooling are all set up in your name from the start. The full stack is yours, working from day one, with no transition project required at any point in the future.

The three phases

Audit. Sprint. Performance partnership.

Each phase has a defined start, defined end, and defined deliverables. You decide whether to continue at every gate, with full transparency on progress and results — so the engagement always reflects where the value clearly is for your business.

01

Phase 1 — Market & Fit Audit · 2–3 weeks · Fixed fee

A bounded, defined-scope engagement. Two to three weeks. We work with you to map the market sizing, competitor positioning, regulatory landscape, channel structure, and customer-research insights for your product in Poland. We pressure-test your value proposition against how Polish buyers actually frame the problem.

Deliverable: a written plan with priorities, KPIs, the 90-day Phase 2 roadmap and the Success Bonus structure for Phase 3. You receive the plan regardless of whether you continue with us. Several clients have taken it and run Phase 2 internally — we would rather be paid honestly for a high-quality audit than secure a Phase 2 that isn't the right fit.

Market sizingCompetitor mapRegulatory scanChannel landscapeWritten 90-day plan
02

Phase 2 — Go-to-Market Sprint · 90 days · Monthly Fixed Cost

Execution of the Phase 1 plan. A flat monthly fee covers our team, AI tooling and project management; media spend is separate and fully transparent. By the end of 90 days: brand localised, Polish website live, first distributors signed or first paid campaigns running, AI reception & content engine active, dashboards reporting weekly.

Cadence: kickoff workshop in week one; weekly written updates every Monday; monthly executive review; one mid-point review at week six where you can change scope without penalty.

03

Phase 3 — Performance Partnership · Ongoing · Fixed + Bonus

The same monthly Fixed Cost continues. When the KPI thresholds agreed in Phase 1 are exceeded, a Success Bonus is paid on top of the base fee — in addition to, never in place of it. You always receive full delivery, and the bonus rewards strong shared performance. The bigger your result, the larger our shared upside.

This is what turns a 90-day launch into a multi-year partnership. Quarterly business reviews replace monthly executive reviews. The dashboard stays live, the team stays consistent, and the Success Bonus thresholds are recalibrated each year as the business grows.
