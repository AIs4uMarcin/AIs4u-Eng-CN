How we launch consumer brands in Poland

Four principles for a successful Polish retail launch.

Polish retail rewards local sophistication: marketplace fluency, the right relationships, authentic content, and a unified view across channels. These four principles guide every consumer-brand launch we run.

Allegro fluency from day one

Allegro’s ranking algorithm rewards Polish-native listings, fast shipping, and review velocity. We set up your marketplace presence the right way from the start - with optimised listings, Smart shipping integration, and active review-generation flows.

Right timing with retail buyers

MediaMarkt, Rossmann, Empik, EuroRTV/AGD and DIY chains run range reviews on six to twelve-month cycles. We know the calendar, the buyers and the category politics - so your brand is presented at the moment when the window is open.

Authentic influencer activation

Polish consumers are sophisticated about sponsored content. We work with mid-tier Polish creators whose audiences trust them, and use AI plus real actors for volume creative - producing campaigns that convert authentically rather than feeling sponsored.

Unified channel architecture

Marketplace, retail and D2C all need to support each other rather than compete. We design the channel architecture - pricing, RRP guardrails, promo discipline - before any channel goes live, so each one reinforces the others under one P&L view.
