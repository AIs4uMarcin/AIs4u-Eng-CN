How we accelerate medical market entry

Four areas where local expertise speeds up the launch.

Polish healthcare is a regulated, payer-driven, relationship-led market. These four areas are where careful local navigation makes the difference between a launch that ships on time and one that is delayed by quarters.

Clear regulatory pathway

URPL submission, Polish-language Instructions for Use and labelling, EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR) compliance, and post-market vigilance setup. We coordinate with local regulatory counsel so the path is fully mapped before scaling commercial spend.

Reimbursement strategy upfront

Whether your product is reimbursed by the Polish national health fund (NFZ), partially refunded, or fully out-of-pocket fundamentally shapes the commercial plan. We work backwards from the reimbursement model to set realistic volume and pricing assumptions from day one.

Compliant HCP marketing

Polish advertising law for healthcare professionals (HCPs) and patient communication for prescription products is strict and specific to the local market. We translate your global messaging into materials that are fully compliant by design, not by retrofit.

Strong KOL & practitioner relationships

Polish healthcare professionals trust their peers and the established medical societies. We engage key opinion leaders (KOLs), head physicians and society stakeholders early - so clinical adoption is supported by trusted voices before patient marketing begins.
