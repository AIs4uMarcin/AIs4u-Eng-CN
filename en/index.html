FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Things foreign teams typically ask us in the first call.

01What does “one point of contact” actually mean in practice?
It means you sign one contract with us, receive one invoice, and work with one named lead — typically Marcin or Magdalena — across all four areas (sales channel, web, brand, campaigns). We coordinate translators, designers, distributors, media partners and lawyers in the background. Your team focuses on strategy while we handle delivery.
02How is the success bonus structured?
The Phase 1 audit produces a written KPI structure — typically a mix of revenue, qualified distributors signed, marketing-qualified leads, or margin improvement. The bonus is paid on top of the monthly Fixed Cost when those thresholds are exceeded. It is capped and transparent. You always receive full delivery, and the bonus rewards strong performance on top of it.
03Do we need a legal entity in Poland?
Not necessarily to start. For distributor and SaaS models you can often operate cross-border under EU rules and your existing HQ entity. For retail, employer-of-record, or VAT-heavy operations, a local entity becomes useful — we’ll flag this in Phase 1 with the trade-offs.
04What does a typical engagement cost?
Phase 1 (the Audit) is a fixed-scope, fixed-fee engagement. Phase 2 is a monthly Fixed Cost scaled to the breadth of your programme — typically more cost-efficient than running multiple agency contracts in parallel. Phase 3 keeps the same monthly Fixed Cost and adds a Success Bonus on top when KPIs are exceeded. We share a clear price band on the first call once we understand the scope.
05Do you cover CEE beyond Poland?
Poland is our home base and the depth of our network. We regularly extend programmes into Czechia, Slovakia, Romania, the Baltics and Hungary using vetted local partners, with the same single-point-of-contact model. We’ll be honest about where we lead versus where we coordinate.
06How do you handle our data and confidentiality?
NDA before any commercial information is shared. GDPR compliance by default. Our CTO holds state-level security clearance and we run our internal AI tooling in closed environments — no client data goes to public LLM providers without an explicit signed scope.
